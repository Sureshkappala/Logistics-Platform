How Chargeable Weight Is Calculated

Freight rates apply to either the actual gross weight or volumetric weight, whichever is higher.

Air Freight

IATA Ratio: 1:6000

Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ 6,000 = Chargeable Volumetric Kg.

1 CBM = 167 kg
Express Courier

Standard Ratio: 1:5000

Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ 5,000 = Chargeable Volumetric Kg.

1 CBM = 200 kg
Ocean LCL

Maritime Ratio: 1:1000

Length × Width × Height (m) = Volume (CBM) billed per revenue ton (1,000 kg).

1 CBM = 1,000 kg (1 Ton)
