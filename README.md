# Stackly - Enterprise Logistics & Supply Chain Platform

A modern, responsive, high-performance Logistics & Transportation web platform built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)** with 0 external frontend dependencies.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌐 Public Marketing Portal
- **Interactive Landing & Service Pages**: Full Truckload (FTL), Less-Than-Truckload (LTL), Temperature-Controlled Cold Chain, Ocean Container Freight, Air Cargo Express, and Customs Clearance.
- **Dynamic Instant Rate Calculator**: Multi-factor linehaul, fuel surcharge, and accessorial cost estimation.
- **Real-Time Public Tracking**: Live milestone checkpoints, GPS coordinates, and telematics telemetry.
- **Enterprise Design System**: Dark & light high-contrast navy theme, custom SVG icons, accessible forms, and mobile responsive drawers.

### 🏢 Customer Shipper Portal
- **Dashboard Overview**: Active shipments monitor, spend analytics, and on-time reliability metrics.
- **Dedicated Track & Book Modules**: Dedicated internal tracking HUD and multi-step cargo dispatch booking wizard.
- **History & Address Book**: Searchable delivery history, saved origin/destination addresses, and enterprise REST API settings.

### 🚚 Fleet Driver Console
- **Navigation & Telematics HUD**: Turn-by-turn route canvas with live GPS vehicle pulse radar.
- **Pickups & Docking Management**: Terminal bay scheduling, geofence check-ins, and digital proof-of-delivery (ePOD).
- **Driver Telemetry & Logs**: Hours of Service (HOS) duty clocks, DVIR pre-trip checklists, and earnings logs.

### 🎛️ Admin Logistics Control Tower
- **Global Operations Cockpit**: Real-time fleet tracking, geofence boundary alerts, and warehouse storage capacity monitors.
- **Roster & Asset Management**: Shipper customer rosters, driver telemetry records, and vehicle diagnostics.
- **Financial & Operational Analytics**: Yield per mile, dwell time indices, and regional freight volume trends.
